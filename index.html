Straight answers

What people ask
before they book.

How much does tree removal cost in Perth?
It comes down to size, access and what is underneath. A small backyard tree with a clear run to the street is at the low end. A large gum overhanging a roof, needing rigging and a crane, is at the high end. We look at the tree first, then give you a fixed price that does not move.
Do I need council approval to remove a tree?
Sometimes. Several Perth councils protect certain species, and trees over a set height or trunk size. We will tell you if we think yours is protected, and we can put together the arborist report the council asks for.
Does home insurance cover tree removal?
Usually only where a tree has already caused damage, or come down in a storm. Routine removal of a healthy tree is almost never covered. Worth a call to your insurer before you book if a tree has already fallen.
Do you take the branches and mulch away?
Yes, and it is in the quote. We chip on site, and you choose: keep the mulch for your garden beds, or we load the lot and take it with us.
Are you insured?
Fully insured and qualified. We are happy to send the certificate of currency through before we start.
Do you do emergency call outs?
Yes, outside working hours for genuine emergencies. A tree down after a storm, a limb over a driveway, anything unsafe. Call rather than email if it is urgent.
